### AyuScout AI — Pharmacovigilance Intelligence Platform

A React dashboard for adverse-event review, safety alerts, KPIs, drug exploration, and report management, backed by FastAPI REST APIs.

**Key capabilities:** patient-identity masking and PII detection · drug-name normalization with fuzzy matching · ICH E2B(R3) XML report exports.

### UrjaNetra AI — Energy Supply-Chain Decision Support

An early-warning concept for import-dependent energy supply chains that combines weather, shipping, commodity, energy-demand, and geopolitical signals.

**Forecasting and decision support:** 24-hour, 3-day, 7-day, and 30-day horizons · LSTM, Transformer, and XGBoost approaches · risk heatmaps, geospatial alerts, and scenario planning.
